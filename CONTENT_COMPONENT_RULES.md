# Content Component Rules

1. Never invent Quran text, hadith, duas, rulings, grading, or citations.
2. A religious content component must support canonical text, corpus/edition, source reference, review state, translation attribution, version, and correction history.
3. Fixture content must be visibly labeled and unusable as religious or timekeeping guidance.
4. Canonical Arabic, translation, transliteration, and editorial explanation are visually and semantically separate.
5. “Verified” may describe component structure only until an actual reviewed source record exists.
6. AI-generated prose can never masquerade as canonical religious content or source evidence.
7. Prayer and Hijri values must display method/authority and qualification when real data is introduced.
8. No promotion, advertising, or engagement mechanic may interrupt Quran, dua, or dhikr reading.
