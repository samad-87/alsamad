# Unresolved Design Decisions

1. Licensed Quran text edition/provider and canonical script.
2. Final Quran-capable Arabic typeface after glyph and waqf-mark QA.
3. Licensed English translation edition and attribution format.
4. Scholarly/editorial review authority and public review-state vocabulary.
5. Final prayer calculation authority, regional defaults, and high-latitude policy.
6. Hijri authority/calculation qualification and local adjustment behavior.
7. Exact bottom-navigation destination set and rollout timing. Mobile First is now a permanent product principle (`ALSAMAD_PRODUCT_ARCHITECTURE_V1.md` §2, `ALSAMAD_SAKINAH_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` §2.12/§25), and a bottom navigation bar is the primary candidate pattern for core mobile destinations; the exact five-item set (illustratively Home, Quran, Search, Journey, Profile) and its activation timing remain pending device testing and the Talibeen Al-Halal release track.
8. Whether bookmarks/authentication remain hidden or ship in Release 1.
9. Offline/PWA caching policy that cannot expose stale prayer times.
10. Audio licensing, reciter catalog, and accessibility behavior.
